## Scope Of The Activity

In the audit's E1–E4 grouping, students develop statistical summaries of center and variation, interpret box plots, construct a box plot from data, and use statistical evidence when discussing design. These are paraphrased scope descriptions, not quoted official objectives.

Mean Machine contributes principally to mean reasoning within E1. Median Depot develops the ordering and middle-value work. Spreadsheet analysis, spread, plot interpretation, documentation, and design discussion still require their own learning evidence. A game-completion message cannot stand in for the whole activity.

The audit's G01–G54 identifiers are document-local analytical targets, not 54 separately published PLTW objectives. **D** retains the audit's classification of a target directly supported by the visible activity. **I** retains its classification of an inferred supporting target. Neither letter means this game has implemented or assessed the target. Use the prefix **DM-1.4-** when referring to an ID elsewhere.

## Accuracy Rules To Carry Forward

- Keep original data visible. Repacking the model does not revise recorded measurements.
- Match receiving bays to original observations. Include duplicates and explicit zeros; missing entries are not zeros.
- Distinguish units of quantity from the number of physical pieces after splitting a block.
- Separate exact calculation from the later rounded report. Keep raw trials and the unit.
- Distinguish mean, median, and the midpoint of the two extremes.
- For class analysis, explicitly choose what one observation represents. The audit proposes one personal mean per student; the underlying activity does not fully specify the class table schema. Do not combine raw trials, means, and best flights in one statistical column.
- State the quartile and whisker conventions when moving to companion plot work. The audit uses median-excluded halves for odd hand-calculation examples and minimum/maximum whiskers. Ties and small datasets require care when describing exact percentages.
- Do not infer individual trial consistency from a plot of student means or infer a design cause from an uncontrolled comparison.
- Keep the broader standards context separate from any claim of current Ohio alignment; no current Ohio crosswalk is established here.
