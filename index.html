Factory Reference

  • Observation
    • One original value. Each labeled pallet stands for one observation.
    • Equal values still count separately. A recorded zero also counts.
  • Quantity
    • One whole ring is one unit; two half-layers together are one unit.
    • Moving or splitting cargo preserves the total and the original records.
  • Mean
    • The equal share: total quantity ÷ number of observations.
    • It can be a value that never appeared in the original data.
  • Median
    • Found by ordering intact observations and locating the middle.
    • Median Depot practices that different action.

In skimmer statistics, one trial is one observation. Repacking this model does not change recorded flight distances.
